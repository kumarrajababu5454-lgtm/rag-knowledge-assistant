Company Leave Policy

Employees receive 18 paid leave days per year.

Employees can request sick leave when they are unable to work because of illness.

Employees should submit planned leave requests at least 3 working days in advance.

Emergency leave can be requested without the normal 3-day notice requirement.

Employees who have completed one year of service are eligible for annual leave.

The manager must approve planned leave requests.

Unused paid leave can be carried forward to the following year, subject to company policy.
